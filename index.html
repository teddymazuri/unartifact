    When filing a bug, please include

    • Browser & version — e.g. Firefox 128.0.3.
    • Operating system — Windows, macOS, Linux distro.
    • Input file type — .doc or .docx, and which app produced it.
    • Which toggles were on — metadata, formatting, boilerplate.
    • The exact log output from the App view's progress panel (copy the whole list).
    • The error text from the browser console (F12 → Console) if the run failed.

    Do not attach the source document unless you're certain it contains nothing private — GitHub issues are public and permanent. A minimal redacted reproduction is almost always more useful than the full file.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 2025

    Short version: your documents never leave your device. UnArtifact is a client-side utility. There is no backend.

    What we process

    When you drop a file, JavaScript reads it into the browser's memory, unzips it, edits its XML, and re-zips it. This happens entirely inside your tab.

    What we collect

    • No file uploads. Zero bytes of your document are transmitted.
    • No telemetry. We do not use analytics, trackers, or fingerprinting.
    • No remote storage. There are no cookies, sessions, or accounts.

    Network requests

    All libraries (JSZip, FileSaver) are bundled locally. Once the page loads, you may disconnect from the internet and the app will continue to function.

    Your rights

    Because we never receive your data, there is nothing to delete, export, or rectify on our side. You retain full control at all times.
